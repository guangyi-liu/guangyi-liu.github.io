Welcome

Hi, welcome to my website! I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Scientist at Amazon Robotics. I obtained my Ph.D. at the Autonomous and Intelligent Robotics (AIR) Laboratory, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Lehigh University, working under the advisory of Prof. Nader Motee.

My research interests are in risk and robustness analysis in networked control systems and perception systems.
